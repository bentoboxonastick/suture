# iOS & iPadOS Navigation and Setup Guide

Suture on iPhone and iPad delivers a responsive, touch-first streaming experience with fluid gestures, Picture-in-Picture, and multitasking.

---

## 📱 Navigation & Gestures

### iPhone (iOS)
- **Bottom Navigation**: Quickly switch between **Home**, **Live TV**, **Search**, and **Settings**.
- **MiniPlayer**: When listening to music or previewing streams, the persistent bottom MiniPlayer allows you to control playback anywhere in the app. Swipe up or tap `^` to expand the fullscreen living room player.
- **Swipe to Dismiss**: Swipe down on any media detail sheet or video player to dismiss.

### iPad (iPadOS)
- **Sidebar Navigation**: Sleek, collapsible sidebar supporting **Stage Manager** and **Split View** multitasking.
- **Picture-in-Picture (PiP)**: Swipe up to go home while streaming to continue watching in a floating PiP window.
- **Pointer / Trackpad Hover**: Media cards elevate and glow on trackpad hover.
