# Apple TV (tvOS) Setup & Living Room Guide

Suture on Apple TV brings the ultimate 10-foot living room media experience with Siri Remote navigation, 4K Dolby Vision playback, and Top Shelf integration.

---

## 📺 System Requirements
- **Apple TV 4K** (1st, 2nd, or 3rd generation) or Apple TV HD.
- **tvOS 18.0** or later.

---

## 🎮 Siri Remote Navigation

| Remote Gesture | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Swipe Clickpad** | Navigate media cards and rails with 3D parallax focus |
| **Click Center** | Select item / Toggle Play & Pause |
| **Circular Scrub on Ring** | Precise high-speed video scrubbing with thumbnail previews |
| **Click Left / Right Edge** | Jump ±10 seconds |
| **Long Press Center** | Open Stream Source selector and external player menu |

---

## 🌟 Top Shelf Setup
When Suture is placed in the top dock row of your Apple TV Home Screen, it displays your **Continue Watching** rail and **Trending Cinema** directly on your TV without needing to launch the app first.
